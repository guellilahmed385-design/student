# Quick Start Guide

Welcome to StudyMate! This guide will help you get started in a minute.

## 1. Open the app
Visit the [StudyMate website](https://guellilahmed385-design.github.io/student/). You can also download the repository and open `index.html` in a modern browser.

## 2. Plan your work
In **Today's tasks**, type a task and press **+**. Tick the checkbox when you finish. The progress bar updates automatically.

## 3. Focus with Pomodoro
Choose **Focus 25**, **Short break 5**, or **Long break 15**. Press **Start focus** to begin, **Ⅱ** to pause, and **↺** to reset. A completed focus session is added to your statistics.

## 4. Revise with flashcards
Enter a question and its answer, then choose **Create flashcard**. Press **Reveal answer** when you are ready, and use the arrows to move between cards.

## 5. Your data and privacy
Your tasks, flashcards, and session count are saved in local storage in your current browser. They stay on that device and browser; they do not sync automatically. Avoid clearing browser site data if you want to keep them.

## Need help?
Share an idea or report a problem through [StudyMate feedback](https://github.com/guellilahmed385-design/student/issues/new/choose).
