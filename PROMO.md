# StudyMate — 20-second demo video

A simple screen-recording plan for introducing StudyMate to classmates.

## Record these scenes

1. **0–3 seconds:** Open the StudyMate home page. Show the name and dashboard.
2. **3–8 seconds:** Click Start focus and show the 25:00 timer.
3. **8–12 seconds:** Add a sample task and tick it as complete; show the progress bar.
4. **12–16 seconds:** Create a flashcard and reveal its answer.
5. **16–20 seconds:** Show the Share StudyMate button and finish on the app link.

Keep the recording vertical (9:16) for WhatsApp Status, Instagram Reels, or TikTok. Use sample study information only; do not show private data or notifications.

## Suggested caption

StudyMate is a free study workspace for students: focus timer, tasks, flashcards, and progress tracking. No account, installation, or API key needed. Try it here:

https://guellilahmed385-design.github.io/student/

## Where to share

- ENSAO class and student groups, where sharing project links is allowed.
- WhatsApp Status and personal social accounts.
- Ask classmates to try one feature and send honest feedback through the Feedback link in the app.
