StudyMate

Make your study sessions count.

A quick guide to using your free StudyMate workspace.

1. Focus timer

Focus 25Work for 25 minutes, then take a break. Choose Short break (5 min) or Long break (15 min). Start, pause, or reset whenever you want.

2. Today's tasks

Write a task and press Add (+). Tick its checkbox when finished. The progress bar updates automatically; use × to remove a task.

3. Flashcards

Enter a question or term, add its answer, and create the card. Press Reveal answer to check what you remember, then use the arrows to move between cards.

4. Progress and privacy

Your focus sessions, tasks, and flashcards are saved in this browser on this device. They are not synced to another device. Clearing browser data may erase them.

5. Share and feedback

Use Share StudyMate to send the app to classmates. If you find a problem or have an idea, use the Feedback link at the bottom of the main page.
